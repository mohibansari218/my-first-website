Happy Birthday Bubu

Aj ka din mere liye sirf aik birthday nhi, balke ye wo din hai jab ap is duniya mein ayin "mere liye" Shayad main kabhi lafzon mein nhi bata paunga ki ap meri zindagi mein kitni ahmiyat rakhti hain. Ap wo hain jo meri har dua mein hain, meri har muskrahat ki wajah hain or mere dil ke sabse kareeb hain.

Jab ap meri zindagi mein ayin, tab mein samjha ki sacha pyaar kia hota hai. Mujhe apni zindagi jeene ka maqsad mil gaya mein ap ke sath zindagi ko bilkul wesi hi jeena chahta hun jesa mene socha hai agar mein apni yaddasht kho bethun or ap ke pas se guzrun to mujhe phir se ishq ho jaye ga .

Mein chahta hun ke ap ki zindagi mein kabhi koi gham na aye, Apka chehra hamesha isi tarah muskurata rahe aur apki aankhon mein hamesha khushiyon ki chamak bani rahe. Jo bhi sapne ap dekhti hain, Allah unhe haqeeqat bana de. Jo bhi manzil ap chahen, wo ap ko mile. Aur jo bhi khushi ap deserve karti hain, wo ap ke kadmon mein ho.

Ap mere liye sirf aik insaan nhi hain, ap meri aadat hain, meri dua hain, meri duniya hain. Agar mujhe zindagi dubara jeene ka mauka mile, toh main har baar apko hi chununga No matter how much i get hurt 🥺.

Kiu ke apke jaisa koi nhi hai aur na hi kabhi ho sakta hai.

Aj apki birthday par mein sirf itna kehna chahta hun ke chahe kitna bhi waqt guzar jaye, meri mohabbat apke liye kabhi kam nahi hogi. Har din ke sath ye or gehri, or khoobsurat hoti jayegi.

Mein hamesha apke sath rehna chahta hun, apki har khushi ka hissa banna chahti hun or apke har sapne ko pura karna chahta hun.

Happy Birthday Meri Bubu, Meri Jaaannnnn, Meri Duniyaaa , Mere Dil Ka Sukoon "Meri har dua mein ap hain, meri har khushi mein ap hain, aur meri zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat kahani bhi ap hain har aik lamha ap ki yaadon mein guzarta hai . Aj ke din bas itna yaad rakhiye, ap bohott special hain ... mere liye sabse zada Meri Piyari Bubu.",

Zindagi mein bohot se log ate hain aur chale jaate hain, par kuch log dil mein aisi jagah bana lete hain jo koi nahi le sakta. Aap mere liye bilkul wesi hi hain.

Aapke sath bitaya hua har lamha hamesha yaadgaar hota hai. Main bas khuda se yahi dua karta hun ke apko duniya ki har khushi mile, ap hamesha muskurati rahen.

Thank you meri life ka aik special hissa banne ke liye. Have the happiest birthday ever! 🎂🎂
